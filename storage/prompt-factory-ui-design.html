Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2025 · Effective: July 1, 2025

1. Data We Collect

When you use PromptFactory, we collect information you provide directly and data generated through your use of the service.

  • Account information: name, email address, profile picture (via Google OAuth)
  • Usage data: prompts viewed, search queries, generated images, session timestamps
  • Payment information: processed securely via Stripe (we do not store card details)
  • Device information: browser type, IP address, operating system

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide, improve, and personalise the PromptFactory service, and to communicate important updates.

  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To enforce daily generation limits and premium tier access
  • To rank prompts by human engagement signals
  • To send product updates (can be unsubscribed at any time)

5. Your Rights (GDPR & DPDP)

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access, rectify, delete, or export your personal data at any time. Submit requests to privacy@promptfactory.io

Engineering Handoff Notes
All CSS variables are defined in :root. Dark-first design — no light mode override needed for v1. Font loading via Google Fonts CDN (Syne + DM Sans). All interactive states (hover, focus, active) are specified in the component stylesheet. Prompt cards use CSS grid with repeat(auto-fill, minmax(240px, 1fr)) for responsive layouts.
